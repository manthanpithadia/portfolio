Scaling Global Connection: The mytamildate Ecosystem

Architecting a high-concurrency dating platform for the global Tamil diaspora.

Type Web App (Mobile-First)
Role Senior Product Developer & MVP Architect
Timeline 8 Weeks (MVP Phase)
Tech Stack Next.js, Node, Redis

report_problem Problem Statement

The legacy platform hit a critical ceiling. With 20k+ concurrent users, the monolithic architecture suffered from frequent database locks, resulting in slow matching and poor user retention.

"Matches took over 5 seconds to load, and the profile verification was manual, creating a 48-hour onboarding bottleneck."

lock_reset

Database Locks at 20k+ Users

Research & Insights

analytics Competitor Analysis

Niche dating apps often suffer from "feature bloat" but lack technical performance. Users in the Tamil diaspora felt that global apps lacked cultural nuance, while local apps were technically unstable.

search_check Usability Analysis

Identified that the profile verification flow had a 65% drop-off rate due to friction. Users wanted a high-trust environment without the friction of a 15-field form.

Design Decisions

touch_app
Swipe-less Discovery

We moved from swiping to a grid discovery to promote "intentional browsing." This reduced accidental matches and increased the high-intent conversation rate by 35%.

bolt
Automated Auditing

Replaced manual profile vetting with an automated AI system. This reduced onboarding friction by 40% while maintaining the "high-trust" community standard.

Lessons Learned

  • check_circle

    Layer Decoupling: Decoupling the caching layer (Redis) from the main matching engine was critical for real-time performance at scale.

  • check_circle

    Cultural Context: Cultural-specific fields (heritage, languages) shouldn't be secondary; they are the primary drivers of compatibility in this niche.
